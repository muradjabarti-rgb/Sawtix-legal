Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sawtix ("Sawtix," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you place a phone order through our AI voice ordering assistant on behalf of a restaurant client.

Information We Collect

  • Your name, as provided during the call
  • Your phone number, used to send you an SMS order confirmation
  • Details of your order (items and total price)

How We Use Your Information

We use this information solely to process your order and send you a text message confirming your order details and total price. Calls may be recorded for quality and compliance purposes.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS Messaging

By providing your phone number during a call with our AI voice ordering assistant, you consent to receive a one-time order confirmation text message. Message and data rates may apply. You will not receive marketing messages from Sawtix via SMS.

Data Retention

Order information is retained only as long as needed to fulfill your order and for basic business record-keeping.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the restaurant where you placed your order, or reach Sawtix directly through the restaurant's Sawtix-powered ordering line.
